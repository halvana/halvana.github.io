Embedded software and hardware integration for interactive kiosk products, including touchscreen systems and connected peripherals.

#### Core Capabilities
- **Core OS**: Embedded Linux & Android operating systems
- **Touch & Display**: Touchscreen driver integration, calibration & multi-touch handling
- **Peripherals**: Printers, barcode scanners, payment modules, cameras & sensors
- **Interfaces**: USB, UART, RS232, I2C, SPI & GPIO peripheral communication
- **Connectivity**: Dual-band Wi-Fi, Bluetooth, and industrial Ethernet
- **Testing & Bring-up**: Hardware bring-up, stress testing, and functional interoperability validation
