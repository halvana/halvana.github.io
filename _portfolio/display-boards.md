Software and hardware integration services for connected display boards, digital signage systems, and commercial display products.

#### Core Capabilities
- **Platforms**: Android & Embedded Linux platform tailoring
- **Display & Audio**: HDMI integration, display panel interfacing
- **Media Pipelines**: High-reliability media playback & streaming engines
- **Connectivity**: Robust Ethernet, Wi-Fi, and cellular network integration
- **Remote Operations**: Remote device management & OTA updates
- **Peripherals & Validation**: Sensor integration, serial peripherals & system reliability validation
