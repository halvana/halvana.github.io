Specialised engineering services for Smart TV platforms covering AOSP, Android TV, embedded Linux, multimedia frameworks, connectivity, HDMI integration, and system-level debugging.

#### Core Capabilities
- **Platform Development**: AOSP, Android TV & Embedded Linux
- **HDMI & Audio**: HDMI-CEC implementation, ARC / eARC audio integration
- **Connectivity**: Bluetooth, Wi-Fi, DLNA & Miracast streaming
- **Media Stack**: Audio/video frameworks, media playback & streaming (HLS, MPEG-DASH, RTSP)
- **System Engineering**: Hardware Abstraction Layer (HAL), device drivers & OTA updates
- **App Integration**: Smart TV application integration, porting & customisation where required
